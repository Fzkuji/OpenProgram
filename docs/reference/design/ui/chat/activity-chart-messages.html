Messages on the work chart

A message block on the work chart has up to three lines: its task's title, what the reader asked, and how it stands. While it goes it says what it is doing now and for how long; once it ended, how long it took and what it did.

The last line

StateLast line
Running a call"Running xelatex pres.tex · 3m 12s": the newest call under way, read from the reply as it streams; a command is named by its first part that is not a cd or an export, without its options or what follows &&; another tool is named in the step's own words, such as "Read notes.md"
Sub-agents going"Running 2 sub-agents · 3m 12s"
No call running"Thinking · 3m 12s", "Writing the reply · 3m 40s", "Retrying (2/3) · 1m 5s": read from the conversation's own copy of the reply as it streams. "Working" when the chart has no copy.
Not started, stopping"Queued", with no time; "Stopping…", "Pausing…"
Waiting on the reader"Waiting for your answer · 2m", "Waiting for approval · 2m", from its last event. What it asked takes the second line, in quotes.
Paused"Paused · 3m 12s", the time it worked
Finished"2m 42s · 4 commands, 2 edits"; with ", 1 failed" in the bright colour when a command, an edit or a sub-agent failed. Reads are not counted.
Ended otherwise"Failed · 41s" with the error as its second line; "Stopped by you · 44s"; "Interrupted · 12s"; a scheduled check's Nothing new, Skipped, Missed, Check failed
  • It never names the last step it took as if it were taking it now.
  • While it goes, waits or is paused, the agent's glyph on it breathes.
  • A topic sums its messages up: "Waiting for you · 2 messages", "Running · 2 messages", "Paused · 2 messages", else the count.

One button, only when it needs the reader

RunOn the block
Waiting for an answer or approvalAnswer, or Review: goes to the composer's card
PausedContinue
Under wayNothing. The composer stops it (its Stop, or Esc); what is typed while it runs is added to it.

Why. Every peer we read keeps run controls off the list and names the one thing under way in the present tense; none mixes tenses as our old "Running Ran · 4 commands" did.

We follow Codex desktop, the closest fit, and keep Continue because a paused run has no composer control.

No stand-ins

  • A message has a title line only once it has a task. Until then it shows what was asked alone.
  • A run nobody asked for is titled once it said something.
  • A message not sorted into a topic yet has a topic block with nothing drawn: room only, no name, not a button. The column's crumb is empty.
  • While no topic has a name yet, the chart starts at the messages column and cannot be moved back to the empty one; once a topic is named, the way back appears.
  • What was asked is its first line with a letter or a digit, so a pasted email's ______ rule is passed over.

What arrives

  • Each new chart glides what moved from where it was, in the slow tier (240 ms) on --ease-standard.
  • What the old chart did not have fades in: a message that starts, a topic that gets its name.
  • A title that arrives on a block already drawn fades in; the lines under it ride down to make room while the block grows.
