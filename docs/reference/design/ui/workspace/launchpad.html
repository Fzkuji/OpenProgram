New-tab Launchpad

The new tab is a launcher laid out and handled like macOS Launchpad: a 7 × 5 grid of icons on pages, a search field, folders and an edit mode. Where Launchpad has an answer, the launcher takes it rather than inventing one.

ActionWhat happens
Two fingers on a trackpadThe pages follow the fingers, at most a page, with a rubber band past the ends. Let go, they settle on the next page if they went far enough or were flicked, else back.
Mouse dragged across empty spaceThe same as two fingers: the pages follow the pointer and settle on release. A press that moves under 6 px stays a click.
Mouse wheelOne notch turns a whole page at once; a held spin turns one page every 280 ms
⌘ arrow, dotA whole page at once
Arrow keys, ReturnMove the selection, crossing to the next page at the row's end, and open it
TypingGoes to the search field; results replace the pages
Option heldThe icons wobble while it is held and stop when it is let go
Long press, or dragging an iconEdit mode, kept until Done, Escape or a click on empty space
Icon draggedMoves at once, no hold first. On another icon's left half it goes before that icon, on its right half after it, and others make room; held on its centre it makes or joins a folder; held at a side or over a dot it turns to that page; past the last page it starts a new one. Let go off the grid after the pages turned, it lands at the end of the page on screen.
FolderOpens over the blurred pages, with its name editable. Dragging an icon out of the panel takes it out of the folder.

Decisions

  • Pages follow the pointer and the fingers, while a wheel notch or key turns a whole page: Launchpad and paging scroll views on macOS do the same, and a desktop pointer drag that does nothing reads as broken.
  • Wheel events do not name their device. A notch is a line or page delta, a fractional pixel delta (macOS mouse acceleration), or an event with nothing after it for 34 ms; a trackpad sends one every frame. A stream that never moved sideways and stops without momentum is a fast-spun wheel and turns a page.
  • Before or after an icon is decided by its middle, so the icon beside the gap stays put until the pointer crosses it; over an icon's body the icons wait 450 ms before making room, so a pointer heading for the centre can make a folder.
  • Dragging an icon starts edit mode without a long press first, as on Launchpad with a mouse.
  • Option shows edit mode only while held, so a quick look at which icons can go needs no Done.
