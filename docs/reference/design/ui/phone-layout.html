Phone layout

At or below 760px the shell is a phone app in one column. The breakpoint is one constant, PHONE_MAX_WIDTH, the same one the secondary rail already used. Everything else is CSS in styles/base/phone.css, loaded last, plus the gestures in phone-shell.tsx. Above 760px nothing changes.

  1. ConversationTab row, one column, composer
  2. Header button or swipe from the left edgeThe sidebar slides over
  3. Tap a chat, the scrim or swipe backThe drawer closes on the chosen chat

Screens

PartOn a phone
SidebarA drawer, min(86vw, 320px) wide, over a --scrim. Opened by the button at the start of the tab row or of a page header, or by a swipe from the left 24px; closed by its own toggle, the scrim, a swipe left, or choosing a chat, page or tab. A drag follows the finger and settles by distance or flick speed. It stays mounted, so it reopens where it was scrolled. No icon rail when closed.
ConversationThe centre card fills the screen; the transcript keeps a 12px margin and the composer lines up with it. The message rail, which lives in the side margin, is not shown; the view controls keep their narrow column, 8px from the top-right corner. Code blocks and tables scroll inside themselves.
Right panelA full-screen sheet sliding in from the right, opened by the button at the end of the tab row and closed by its toggle or by choosing a tab. Tool details, files, activity and resources use the whole width.
Settings, History, Projects, AgentsThe secondary rail is the page's section list, full width; a row opens its content. The list page's header has only the sidebar button; the content page's only a back chevron to the list.
Management pagesDrawer button, rail toggle and title share the first header row; tabs and the toolbar wrap below. The 62px reserve for the collapsed rail is gone.
Composer and question cardsThe three-part composer keeps its layout and spans the column; question and approval cards live in it, so they are full width too.

Touch and screen edges

RuleHow
KeyboardThe shell is position: fixed at the visual viewport's top and height (--phone-viewport-top, --phone-viewport-h from visualViewport), falling back to 100dvh, so the composer rides above the on-screen keyboard in Safari too.
Notch and home indicatorThe shell pads by env(safe-area-inset-*); the drawer and the sheet pad by at least 8px or the inset.
Touch targetsHeader buttons, drawer and sheet rows, and section-list rows are 44px.
InputsAt least 16px, so focusing one does not zoom the page.
HoverHover tips never open from a touch; message actions are already visible without hover.
Stored choicesOpening the drawer, the sheet or a section page on a phone stores nothing, and a stored open right panel starts closed: a wider window keeps its own choices.
MotionDrawer and sheet slide in 220ms; a drag follows the finger; reduced motion removes the slides.

Taken from dsh-mobile

The design copies saya-ch/dsh-mobile (Apache-2.0), a touch layout for another harness's web UI, rather than inventing one.

DecisionWhy
Session list as an overlay drawer with scrimThe conversation keeps the whole width; the list is one swipe away and keeps its scroll position.
Right panel as a full-screen sheetA 300px column leaves no room for text on a phone; a sheet shows details at full width.
Settings sections as a list page with a way backThe section list and its content cannot share 390px.
Keyboard-following height, safe-area padding, 44px targets, 16px inputsThe checklist from its design system: primary actions above the keyboard, nothing under the notch, no focus zoom.
No page-level sideways scroll; wide content scrolls in placeSame.

Not copied: its folded 56px rail beside the conversation (OpenProgram keeps no rail on a phone) and its tablet docking band (at 761px and up the desktop layout already applies).

Implementation status

The page's viewport declares viewport-fit=cover. interactive-widget=resizes-content is not declared yet; the visual-viewport height covers the keyboard without it.
