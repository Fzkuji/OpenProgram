statusCauseRecovery
readyusablenone
cooling429: held until the provider's reset, else 60 s doubling per repeat up to 15 minautomatic when the hold ends, or the next success
quota402, or any refusal whose body says the credit or plan is spent (CreditsError, insufficient_quota, "insufficient balance", Alibaba's 400 Arrearage)held until the quota reader's reset, else 24 h; a passing check clears it
invalid_key401 or 403 on a key, whose body does not say the credit is spentreplace the key
needs_login401 or 403 on a sign-in after one refresh, or a refused refresh tokensign in again

A credential's status, cooldown_until_ms and last_error ({at, code, message}) are its whole state. A model the key may not use is blocked on that key alone (blocked_models), not as a status.

The body decides before the status code. OpenCode Zen answers a zero balance with 401 CreditsError; read as a refused key, the page would say "Replace the key" when the fix is a top-up. The phrasings live in one list, errors.py BILLING_MARKERS, taken from the adapters' bodies and the peers' billing tables (hermes-agent _BILLING_PATTERNS, openclaw failover-matches).

A switch adds one muted line to the conversation. When nothing is usable, the error lists every credential's state in plain words ("rate limited", "out of quota", "key refused", "sign in again").

Why a hold, and only for these

A limited or spent key must not be sent again on the next request, or every turn pays one refused round trip before switching. A 5xx or a 404 says nothing about the key, so it is never held.
