Use OpenProgram from other devices

  1. Add deviceSettings → Devices: pick what it is
  2. Scan or pasteA one-time code, good for 10 minutes
  3. Signed inUntil you revoke it, or it goes unused for 30 days

Conversations, files and accounts stay on the computer that runs them. A phone or browser shows that computer's OpenProgram; another computer's OpenProgram lists its conversations beside its own and sends what you do there. Every device, phones and servers alike, is one list under Settings → Devices.

A code works once and is replaced while it is shown. If nothing can reach this computer yet, Add device offers to switch on Same Wi-Fi.

Reaching the address is not enough to sign in: every device is added with a code first. The link carries every address that is on, and the other side tries each in turn, so a laptop that leaves home keeps working.

On the phone

  1. Open the linkFrom the QR code, through Anywhere
  2. Add to Home ScreenShare → Add to Home Screen; Chrome: Install app
  3. Turn on notificationsSettings → General → Notifications

Opened from the Home Screen, OpenProgram fills the screen like an app. Notifications come from this computer when a conversation needs you, a reply is ready or a conversation stops with an error; each can be turned off.

Tapping one opens that conversation. They say only which kind of event it was, never what the conversation says. They need Anywhere: a phone on Same Wi-Fi opens OpenProgram but gets no notifications.

This computer's permissions

Choose This computer in the list to see what OpenProgram may use here. Turn on opens the right place in System Settings with a small window under it showing the app to drag into the list; the row turns on by itself. Controlling another app and opening files are asked by macOS the first time they are needed.
