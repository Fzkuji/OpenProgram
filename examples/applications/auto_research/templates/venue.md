# Venue

## Venue
Name, track, deadline (with time zone), page limit, style file and options.

## Requirements
Every rule from the call for papers and the author's own requirements, one per line.

## Submission checklist
Only what no task checks. paper_check covers style, page limit and required sections;
citation_check the references; code_release and deliverables what rules.json lists.
- [ ] Anonymous: no names, affiliations, acknowledgements or identifying links
- [ ] Title and abstract in the submission system match the paper

## Camera-ready checklist
- [ ] Reviewer comments addressed or answered
- [ ] Title, abstract and authors in the submission system match the paper
- [ ] Poster, slides or video requirements of the venue copied into deliverables in rules.json
