# text_overlap task

The `text_overlap` task of auto_research (`tasks/overlap/`). A pre-submission self-check, learned from Turnitin, iThenticate and Paperpal's similarity reports and their self-plagiarism checks: it finds passages of the paper that repeat the author's earlier papers, the works the paper cites, or the closest works on its topic. It is not a replacement for the venue's iThenticate check, and the report says so.

| Item | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | List every passage of the paper that repeats a source, with where it is, what it repeats, and whether it is quoted or cited; give a similarity index. |
| Input | `paper_dir`: the paper's Git repository; `workflow/rules.json` names the main `.tex` (default `0main.tex`). `own_papers`: comma-separated `.tex`/`.pdf`/`.txt`/`.md` files or folders of the author's earlier papers, relative to `paper_dir` or absolute; every such file under `workflow/own_papers/` is added. `out_dir`: the report folder (default `<paper_dir>/drafts/text_overlap`). A missing repository or main `.tex`, a main `.tex` without prose, or an `own_papers` path that does not exist raises `ValueError`. |
| Prose | `prose.py`: the document between `\begin{document}` and the bibliography, `\input`/`\include` followed, as sentences with the `file:line` each starts on. Comments, math, citations, labels, references, display environments and tables are dropped; `\emph`, `\footnote`, `\caption` text stays. Each sentence records whether a `\cite` stands in it and which words are inside quotation marks or `\enquote`. |
| Sources | `gather.py`. Own: the files above (PDFs read with pypdf). Reference: each bibliography entry that resolves to arXiv (eprint or arXiv DOI, else an arXiv title search that matches the title closely), at most 30; full text from arXiv HTML or ar5iv through `../survey/fulltext.py`, cached in `<paper_dir>/drafts/text_overlap/cache/`, else the abstract. Entries not on arXiv (`library.py`, learned from Wiley's AI Gateway and Unpaywall): a PDF the author has in `workflow/papers/` or Zotero storage (`ZOTERO_STORAGE`, else `~/Zotero/storage`; at most 400 PDFs indexed by first page), matched by citation key or DOI in the file name or the title on its first page — how subscribed papers are read — else an open-access PDF from the locations OpenAlex records for the DOI or same-title records, or the `citation_pdf_url` of a landing page. Search: abstracts of the top 10 arXiv and Semantic Scholar results for the paper's `\title`, without the paper itself and works already compared. |
| Method | `match.py`. Words lowercased, punctuation stripped, whitespace collapsed; word 8-gram shingles. A sentence matches a source when at least half its words lie in 8-grams the source shares; adjacent matching sentences merge into a passage; a passage of at least 25 words with at least 60% of its own 8-grams in the source is a match. Similarity index = words of the paper inside matches / all words. |
| Commercial database ("商业查重库") | `providers.py`, `turnitin.py`, `commercial.py`. Optional, learned from iThenticate, Turnitin, Copyleaks and Originality.ai: the paper is also checked against a commercial plagiarism database (web pages, publisher content), but only with a key and the author's consent. Providers: see the table below. Winston AI, `POST https://api.gowinston.ai/v2/plagiarism` ([docs](https://docs.gowinston.ai/api-reference/v2/plagiarism/post); key `WINSTONAI_API_KEY` or `WINSTON_API_KEY` from dev.gowinston.ai, free credits on sign-up, 2 credits a word; chunks of at most 100,000 characters, the API takes 100 to 120,000) and Originality.ai, `POST https://api.originality.ai/api/v3/scan` with only `check_plagiarism` on and `storeScan` off ([docs](https://docs.originality.ai/scan); key `ORIGINALITY_API_KEY`, Enterprise plan; chunks of at most 10,000 characters because one plagiarism scan can take 60 s; 500 requests a minute). `workflow/rules.json` `notes.plagiarism_provider` names one (`winston`, `originality`, `turnitin`) or turns the check off (`none`); otherwise the first with a key is used. A provider without a key is named "not configured" and the status does not change. Consent: the line `Send to plagiarism service: yes` in `workflow/text_overlap.md`; when a key is set and the line is missing, it is added as `no` and nothing is sent, and the report says how to opt in. Winston AI and Originality.ai get only the prose, as `prose.py` reads it: never the bibliography, comments, math or citation keys; Turnitin gets the compiled PDF (below). Requests time out after 120 s; 429 (after `Retry-After`), 5xx and network errors are retried 3 times; any other error is named in the report and the run goes on (`PASS_WITH_NOTES`). A source counts as cited when the service says so or it matches a bibliography entry (arXiv id, DOI or title); a source with the paper's own title (its preprint) is shown but never fails. **Thresholds**: `FAIL` when the provider's overall similarity is at least 25%, or one uncited source covers at least 5% of the words sent, close to the limits venues and journals commonly apply to iThenticate similarity reports (overall around 20 to 30%, a single source around 5%). Copyleaks (results only by webhook) is not supported. |
| Output | JSON with `status`, `report`, `json`, `run`, `index`, `matches` (location, words, overlap %, source, kind, url, quoted, cited, noted, passage cut to 300 characters), `sources` (how many were compared), `unavailable_sources` and `commercial` (provider, status `done`/`not_configured`/`off`/`not_opted_in`/`eula_required`/`error`, score, number of matches, providers not configured, Turnitin submission id). `FAIL`: a match with another work and no `\cite` in its sentences, or more than 100 words from an own paper with neither a `\cite` in the passage nor a line in `workflow/text_overlap.md` naming the file. `PASS_WITH_NOTES`: other matches, or a source or look-up that could not be read. A commercial database problem (above) is also `FAIL`; its matches, a failed request or a pending Turnitin EULA make `PASS_WITH_NOTES`. `PASS`: no match and every source read. `overlap.json` has the section `commercial` with every provider match (url, title, matched words, percent, excerpt, cited, same paper) and its problems; `report.md` the section 商业查重库. Files in `<out_dir>/<timestamp>/`: `report.md` (Chinese), `overlap.json` (every match with its whole passage, every source), `run.json`, `run.md`. |
| Effects | Reads the paper, its bibliographies and the own papers. Sends requests to the public APIs of arXiv and Semantic Scholar to arXiv HTML / ar5iv, and to OpenAlex and the open-access PDFs it lists; reads PDFs in workflow/papers/ and Zotero storage; with a provider key and the author's consent, sends the prose to that plagiarism service (paid by credits), or for Turnitin the compiled PDF, creating one submission in the institution's tenant that is not indexed and never deleted or changed. Writes only the report folder and the cache, and, when a provider key is set and the consent line is missing, adds `Send to plagiarism service: no` to `workflow/text_overlap.md`. |
| Model calls | None. |
| Failure | A source that fails is named in `unavailable_sources` and the run goes on. Rerunning is safe; cached full texts are reused. |
| Tests | `tests/overlap/test_workflow.py`: a fixture paper with one passage from a fake own paper and one from a fake arXiv source, the network replaced. `tests/overlap/test_commercial.py`: the Winston AI and Originality.ai request and answer shapes with HTTP faked, consent, provider choice, chunking, missing keys, refused requests and retries. `tests/overlap/test_turnitin.py`: a fake TCA tenant for every step (features, create, PDF and text upload, processing and report polling, viewer URL), EULA required / accepted at Turnitin / accepted by the line / refused with 451, the poll timeout, 4xx refusals with the submission id, 5xx retries. |
| Usage | `from auto_research.tasks.overlap import text_overlap`; `text_overlap("<repo>", own_papers="~/papers/workshop.pdf")`. Not yet in `registry.py`, so `openprogram programs run ... -a task=text_overlap` does not reach it. |

## Commercial providers

| Provider | Request | Configuration | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winston AI (`winston`) | `POST https://api.gowinston.ai/v2/plagiarism`, one answer per request ([docs](https://docs.gowinston.ai/api-reference/v2/plagiarism/post)) | `WINSTONAI_API_KEY` or `WINSTON_API_KEY` | the prose, in chunks of at most 100,000 characters |
| Originality.ai (`originality`) | `POST https://api.originality.ai/api/v3/scan`, one answer per request ([docs](https://docs.originality.ai/scan)) | `ORIGINALITY_API_KEY` | the prose, in chunks of at most 10,000 characters |
| Turnitin / iThenticate (`turnitin`) | Turnitin Core API (TCA), the REST API of iThenticate 2.0 and Turnitin Similarity integrations ([API reference](https://developers.turnitin.com/docs/tca), customer login; [FAQ](https://developers.turnitin.com/turnitin-core-api/faq); [EULA workflow](https://developers.turnitin.com/turnitin-core-api/best-practice/eula-workflow)) | `TCA_BASE_URL` (the institution's tenant, `https://<tenant>.turnitin.com/api/v1`; `/api/v1` is added when missing), `TCA_API_KEY` (an API key of an integration scope, from the institution's Turnitin admin), optional `TCA_OWNER` (the user id the submission belongs to; default a stable id for this computer's user) | the compiled PDF of the main `.tex` (`<main>.pdf` next to it, compile first), else the prose as a `.txt` |

Turnitin flow, as the API reference documents it, with polling instead of a webhook: `GET /features-enabled` (whether the tenant requires the EULA, which repositories are licensed); when required, `GET /eula/latest` and `GET /eula/{version}/accept/{owner}`; `POST /submissions` (owner, submitter, title, `USER` permission sets, the EULA record when the author gave one); `PUT /submissions/{id}/original` (`binary/octet-stream`, `Content-Disposition: inline; filename="…"`); `GET /submissions/{id}` until `COMPLETE`; `PUT /submissions/{id}/similarity` (`add_to_index: false`; repositories INTERNET, PUBLICATION, CROSSREF, CROSSREF_POSTED_CONTENT as far as licensed; `exclude_quotes` and `exclude_bibliography`); `GET /submissions/{id}/similarity` until `COMPLETE` (`overall_match_percentage`, per-repository percentages, `top_matches` with name, percentage, source type and matched words; no URLs or excerpts); `POST /submissions/{id}/viewer-url` (a short-lived link to the full report). Every request carries `Authorization: Bearer <TCA_API_KEY>`, `X-Turnitin-Integration-Name` and `X-Turnitin-Integration-Version`. Polling starts at 5 s and grows to 60 s; after 10 minutes in all the run stops waiting (`error`, the submission id kept in the report) and the local check goes on. 400, 401, 403, 404, 409 (`CANNOT_EXTRACT_TEXT`, `TOO_LITTLE_TEXT`), 413 and a submission `ERROR` with its `error_code` are named in the report; 429 and 5xx are retried. The submission id is recorded in `overlap.json` and the report; the submission is never deleted or changed and is not added to the institution's index, so the venue's own iThenticate check does not match the paper against this self-check.

**EULA.** Turnitin requires everyone who submits to have seen and accepted its End User License Agreement. When the tenant requires it and Turnitin has no acceptance for the owner, nothing is sent: the status is `eula_required` (`PASS_WITH_NOTES`) and the report gives the EULA's URL and version. The program never accepts it for the author. After reading it, the author who agrees writes `Turnitin EULA accepted: <version>` in `workflow/text_overlap.md`; the next run sends that acceptance (version, language, time) with the submission. A new EULA version needs a new line. A `451` refusal from Turnitin is treated the same way.

## Not covered

- Paywalled works the author has not downloaded: compared only when an open-access copy exists.
- Paraphrase: shingles find copied wording, not rewritten ideas.
- Figures, tables and equations.
